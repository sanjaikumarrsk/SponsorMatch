# Sponsor Match — Prove. Create. Collaborate.

> **Don’t Just Show Your Reach. Show Your Impact.**

Sponsor Match is a sponsorship marketplace that helps brands discover the right creators based not only on follower count, but on **real-world impact, proven results, trust, and creative ideas**.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Brands, especially small and medium-sized businesses, often struggle to find creators who genuinely match their target audience, location, campaign goals, and budget.

At the same time, talented small creators are overlooked because traditional influencer discovery often emphasizes **followers and reach** rather than actual results.

This creates a gap:

- Brands waste time searching for suitable creators.
- Small businesses may not have the resources for expensive influencer platforms.
- Smaller creators struggle to gain visibility.
- Follower count does not always represent real business impact.
- Creators spend time creating pitches that may never get reused.
- Brands lack a structured way to compare creators, ideas, and previous results.

## 💡 Our Solution

Sponsor Match creates a structured ecosystem where:

**Brands → Discover → Compare → Collaborate**

**Creators → Prove → Pitch → Collaborate → Build Credibility**

Instead of treating follower count as the primary measure of a creator, Sponsor Match gives brands access to **receipts, verified outcomes, collaboration history, community vouches, and creative pitches**.

---

# 🔥 Core Innovation — Receipts, Not Reach

The main innovation of Sponsor Match is **“Receipts, Not Reach.”**

Creators can showcase evidence of the impact they have created through previous promotions and collaborations.

Examples of receipts:

- Customer DMs
- Coupon redemptions
- Store visits / footfall
- Purchase or conversion evidence
- Business testimonials
- Campaign results
- Customer responses
- Word-of-mouth results
- Screenshots and supporting evidence

For example:

A creator with **45K followers** may have no evidence of actual business impact.

Another creator with only **1.2K followers** may have multiple receipts showing that their content generated real customer visits and conversions.

Sponsor Match allows the brand to see that difference.

> **Followers show potential reach. Receipts show actual impact.**

Creators can also request businesses to verify their submitted receipts.

Verification status can be:

- Creator Submitted
- Verification Requested
- Brand Verified ✓
- Verification Rejected

This makes creator profiles more meaningful than simply displaying follower numbers.

# 🚀 Key Innovations

## 1. Receipts, Not Reach

Creators showcase real evidence of previous impact instead of relying only on follower count.

## 2. Brand Verification

Businesses can verify creator-submitted impact claims, making receipts more trustworthy.

## 3. Brand Announcements → Creative Pitches

Brands can publish specific campaign opportunities.

An announcement can include:

- Campaign objective
- Target audience
- Location
- Category
- Content requirements
- Campaign duration
- Deadline
- Budget
- Paid / Barter / Both
- Desired impact
- Required receipt types

Creators can respond with structured creative pitches containing:

- Campaign idea
- Story/concept
- Content format
- Brand integration
- Target audience
- Expected impact
- Execution plan
- Timeline
- Pricing/request
- Previous work
- Relevant receipts

This allows brands to compare **ideas + impact + credibility**, rather than just profiles.

## 4. Second-Chance Pitch

A rejected pitch does not have to become wasted work.

Creators can:

**Rejected Pitch → Reuse Pitch → Find Similar Opportunities → Adapt → Resubmit**

This makes creative effort reusable and reduces pitch fatigue.

## 5. Community Vouching

After a successful collaboration, a brand can give a collaboration-linked vouch to the creator.

Example:

> “Delivered the campaign on time and generated excellent customer response.”  
> — ABC Café ✓ Verified Collaboration

Receipts answer:

**“Can this creator create results?”**

Vouches answer:

**“Can I trust this creator to work professionally?”**

## 6. Split / Bundle Collaboration

Brands can collaborate with multiple smaller creators instead of spending the entire budget on one creator.

Example:

**₹10,000 campaign budget**

→ 5 creators × ₹2,000

This gives brands access to multiple communities, styles, and locations while creating opportunities for more small creators.

## 7. Barter Matching

Not every sponsorship needs to be monetary.

Sponsor Match supports:

- Paid
- Barter
- Both

Examples:

- Café → Free meals for food content
- Gym → Free membership for fitness content
- Hotel → Free stay for travel content

This is especially useful for small businesses with limited marketing budgets.

## 8. Milestone Collaboration

Brands and creators can structure collaborations around milestones.

Example:

**₹10,000 Collaboration**

- ₹5,000 after content delivery
- ₹5,000 after campaign completion

The prototype focuses on milestone status tracking rather than real payment processing.
